# Data Retention Policy

## Application logs
Application logs are retained for 30 days unless a legal hold requires longer retention. Access to raw logs is limited to authorized engineering and security personnel.

## Customer exports
Customer data exports are stored for 7 days and then automatically deleted. An export must not be shared through public links.

## Deletion requests
Verified deletion requests are reviewed by the data owner and completed within 14 calendar days, unless a legal or contractual requirement prevents deletion.
