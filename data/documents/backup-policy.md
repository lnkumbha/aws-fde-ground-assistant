# Backup and Recovery Policy

## Backup schedule
Production databases are backed up every 24 hours. Backups are encrypted at rest and access is restricted to the platform operations team.

## Recovery objectives
The target recovery point objective (RPO) is 24 hours. The target recovery time objective (RTO) is 4 hours for the primary application.

## Restore testing
A restore test is performed quarterly. Results and identified gaps are recorded in the operational review.
