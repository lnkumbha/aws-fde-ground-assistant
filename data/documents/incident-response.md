# Incident Response Procedure

## Initial triage
The on-call engineer acknowledges a high-severity incident within 15 minutes. The incident commander records impact, timeline, and decisions in the incident channel.

## Customer communication
The incident commander coordinates customer updates with the account owner. Do not disclose unverified root causes.

## Post-incident review
A post-incident review is scheduled within 5 business days after a high-severity incident is resolved. Action items must have an owner and due date.
